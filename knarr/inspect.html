Listen to the highlighted word, then check its voice source in the views below, which share one time axis (0 = word start). Creak shows as widely and irregularly spaced glottal pulses: uneven gaps in the waveform and the period plot, vertical striations far apart or uneven in the wideband spectrogram, pitch dropping low or scattering. A word is creaky when any part of it is creaky; if only part of it is, drag across that part and press K to mark it. Drag across any view to select a stretch, then zoom to it or play it (also at half speed, where single pulses become audible). /*NOTE*/
